# Vision Analyzer Skill

Turn any uploaded image into actionable business intelligence.

## What Claude Can See and Analyze

- Website screenshots → conversion rate optimization feedback
- Marketing materials → messaging and design critique
- Thumbnails and social graphics → click-through rate improvements
- Documents and PDFs → content extraction and analysis
- Competitor screenshots → tactical intelligence
- Data visualizations → interpretation and insight
- Real photos → product, space, or situational analysis

## the user's Business Context for Vision Analysis

When analyzing images, always apply this context:

**the user's business context:**
- Goal: Convert visitors into mortgage leads
- Audience: Michigan homebuyers, first-time buyers, refinancers
- Brand: Professional, trustworthy, accessible, expert
- CTA always: Contact us, get a quote, apply now

**StanleyAI context:**
- Goal: Drive GitHub stars and developer adoption
- Audience: Developers, indie hackers, non-technical builders
- Brand: Practical, open-source, built by a real person
- CTA always: Star the repo, try it, contribute

## Analysis Frameworks by Image Type

### Website / Landing Page Screenshots
Analyze for:
1. Above-the-fold clarity — is the value prop obvious in 3 seconds?
2. Call-to-action visibility and copy
3. Trust signals (reviews, credentials, logos)
4. Mobile responsiveness issues
5. Conversion friction points
6. Headline strength
7. Social proof placement

Output: Prioritized list of improvements, ranked by impact on conversions

### Thumbnails and Social Graphics
Analyze for:
1. Readability at small sizes
2. Color contrast and visual hierarchy
3. Text to visual ratio
4. Emotional hook and curiosity gap
5. Face and eye direction (if applicable)
6. Brand consistency
7. A/B test suggestions

Output: Specific changes with reasoning, plus 2-3 alternative direction suggestions

### Marketing Materials (Flyers, Ads, Rate Sheets)
Analyze for:
1. Message clarity — what's the one thing this communicates?
2. Audience targeting accuracy
3. Call to action strength
4. Design hierarchy
5. Trust and credibility signals
6. Legal compliance flags (for mortgage materials)

### Documents and Screenshots
Extract:
- Key data points and numbers
- Structured information
- Action items or decisions
- Comparison opportunities

## How to Use This Skill

1. Upload any image to the chat
2. Tell me what you want: "What should I change to get more leads?" or "Analyze this for CTR" or "What's wrong with this design?"
3. Or say nothing — I'll apply the most relevant business context automatically and give you a full analysis
