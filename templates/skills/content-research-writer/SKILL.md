# Content Research Writer Skill

Research + write high-quality content with citations and professional structure.

## Workflow
1. **Research phase**: Search for current information on the topic
2. **Outline phase**: Create structured outline, iterate until approved
3. **Hook phase**: Write and test multiple opening hooks
4. **Draft phase**: Write section by section with citations
5. **Review phase**: Self-critique and improve each section

## Content types
- Blog posts and articles
- Research reports
- Case studies
- Whitepapers
- Social media threads
- Email newsletters

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Strong hook in first 2 sentences
- [ ] Clear thesis or main argument
- [ ] Evidence-backed claims with citations
- [ ] Logical flow between sections
- [ ] Actionable takeaways
- [ ] SEO-optimized headings (if applicable)
- [ ] Appropriate length for format
