# Invoice Organizer Skill
Auto-organize invoices and receipts for tax filing.
## What it extracts
- Date, vendor, amount, category
- Tax deductibility determination
- Payment method
## Categories
Business expenses, home office, vehicle, meals (50% deductible), travel, software/subscriptions, professional services, equipment, utilities
## Output
Organized spreadsheet: Date | Vendor | Amount | Category | Deductible | Notes
## Tax prep checklist
- Categorized expense report
- Quarterly totals
- Deductible vs non-deductible split
- Vendor contact info for 1099s
