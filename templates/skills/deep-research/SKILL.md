# Deep Research Skill

Delegate complex research tasks to Gemini Deep Research for thorough, multi-source analysis.

## When to use
- Market analysis and competitive intelligence
- Literature reviews
- Due diligence research
- Trend analysis
- Any research requiring 10+ sources

## How to invoke Gemini Deep Research
This skill works via the Gemini API with deep_research mode enabled.

## Research workflow
1. Define the research question clearly
2. Identify the scope (sources, depth, timeframe)
3. Structure the output format needed
4. Execute via Gemini Deep Research
5. Synthesize and present findings

## Output structure
- Executive summary
- Key findings (ranked by relevance)
- Source citations
- Gaps and limitations
- Recommended next steps
